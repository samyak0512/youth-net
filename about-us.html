About Us

A non-profit built by young Naga professionals, for young people

Registered under the Registration of Societies Act, 1860, YouthNet was launched on 1 February 2006 and has grown into one of the leading youth organisations in Northeast India.

Our story

YouthNet began in 2006, when a small group of young Naga professionals decided that Nagaland's youth needed more than good intentions — they needed structured, sustained support to turn ambition into livelihood. What started as a single desk in Dimapur has since grown into a region-wide organisation registered under the Registration of Societies Act, 1860.

Today, YouthNet is regarded as one of the premier youth organisations in Northeast India, working to address education, unemployment, entrepreneurship and livelihood challenges — always in partnership with government bodies, grassroots communities, educational institutions, corporations and philanthropic partners.

Our mission

To help youth acquire knowledge, develop life skills and form the attitudes that let them become self-directing, positive, productive and contributing members of society, through active participation and involvement.

Our vision

A future where every young person is driven and empowered toward sustainability, development and productivity.

Our objective

To work in partnership with government, community, grassroots organisations, educational institutions, corporations and philanthropists across every aspect of development, livelihood, skill-building and entrepreneurship — with young people actively engaged in the process, not just as beneficiaries of it.
