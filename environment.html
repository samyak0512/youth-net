Our Work / Environment
Environment

Community-led environmental action across the hills

Watershed protection, waste management and climate-resilient farming led by the communities who depend on the land.

Environment led by communities

YouthNet's environmental work is grounded in the belief that conservation succeeds when the communities closest to the land lead it. Our programmes combine climate leadership training for youth with practical forest and watershed management on the ground, often delivered with international partners.
