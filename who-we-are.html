Who We Are

Twenty years of youth-led change in the Northeast

YouthNet is a non-profit founded in 2006 by a group of young Naga professionals, working across Education, Employment & Livelihood, Entrepreneurship and Environment to help young people in Nagaland and the wider Northeast build self-directed, productive lives.
