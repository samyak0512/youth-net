North East Entrepreneurship Fellowship (N.E.E.F.)

North East Entrepreneurship Fellowship is the brainchild of NorthEast Leaders Connect, an initiative of IEF Foundation and YouthNet. An annual fellowship program that brings together the brightest and high growth entrepreneurs from across the region. Each fellowship consists of entrepreneurs from all 8 states of the North East, India, to come together for a yearlong fellowship of knowledge and exposure, networking opportunity and financial access.
