NELC

Northeast Leaders Connect — one Northeast, one heartbeat

A landmark YouthNet initiative, launched in 2011 in Kohima, that brings young leaders and achievers from across the Northeast to a single platform.

What NELC is

Northeast Leaders Connect (NELC) — earlier known as Young Leaders Connect — is YouthNet's flagship regional convening. It provides a common platform for young leaders and achievers from all 8 Northeast states, with the goal of building a stronger, more connected network across the region.

Each edition brings together change-makers from business, politics, academia, sport, music and culture to plan a roadmap for the Northeast — and to strategise on interventions at both the grassroots and policy level. Past editions have convened leaders spanning sport, entertainment and public life from across all eight states.

NELC has been held annually since its first edition in Kohima in 2011, and continues to be organised in partnership with regional publishers and institutions.
