Our Milestone

Twenty years, one district at a time

From a single youth desk in Dimapur to a movement spanning the Northeast — the moments that shaped how YouthNet works today.

2006

YouthNet is founded

Launched on 1 February 2006 by a group of young Naga professionals and registered under the Registration of Societies Act, 1860, with a single desk in Dimapur.

2011

Northeast Leaders Connect is launched

YouthNet convenes the first Young Leaders Connect in Kohima, bringing changemakers from business, politics, academia, sports, music and culture from across the Northeast to one table.

2013

Impact 5000 by 18 & first skill mela

In partnership with the Government of Nagaland, YouthNet launches the 'Impact 5000 by 18' livelihood campaign, and co-runs a job-oriented skill development mela in Dimapur with over 7,000 candidates registering.

2015

Reach expands to all districts & national recognition

Education and Livelihood programmes extend into every district of Nagaland then in existence, and 'Impact 5000 by 18' receives a special mention at the Northeast Social Impact Awards.

2018

Inspire Awards & placement recognition

YouthNet is recognised with the Inspire Awards 2018–19 and the Best Performance Award at the Placement Officers Meet organised by Quest Alliance, Bangalore.

2020

Relief work at regional scale

YouthNet mobilises emergency relief and continuity-of-livelihood support across the Northeast during the pandemic years.

2022

Nagaland MSME Champion

Honoured by the State Government for enterprise incubation and Zero Defect, Zero Effect (ZED) certification support to Nagaland MSMEs.

2023

Skill India Award

Recognised by the Ministry of Skill Development as Best Training Partner for placement outcomes across the Northeast.

2025

Digital Impact Leadership Award

Honoured at the first Nagaland Influencers Summit 2025 in Kohima for work strengthening the state's youth and digital ecosystem.

2026

294,300 lives, and counting

Two decades on, YouthNet's work spans all 17 districts of Nagaland and all 8 Northeast states, with recognition at the North East Leadership Award 2026 and the Nagaland SDG Action Awards.
