Our Work / Employment & Livelihood
Employment & Livelihood

Connecting Nagaland's youth to real, sustainable jobs

Skilling, placement and livelihood support that turns training into employment — and employment into a lasting livelihood.

From training to livelihood

Employment & Livelihood is YouthNet's largest area of work by reach. It spans job placement through the YouthNet Job Centre, structured skilling through the YouthNet Skill Development Centre, and livelihood generation programmes for communities where formal jobs are scarce.

The YouthNet Job Centre (YJC) alone has helped more than 2,400 candidates find employment — from operations and digital marketing roles to hospitality, food & beverage, and retail positions across Dimapur and Kohima.

Key initiatives

Programmes under Employment & Livelihood

Placement

YouthNet Job Centre (YJC)

Registers, trains and places job seekers — freshers and experienced candidates alike — into roles across Dimapur and Kohima. Over 2,400 candidates placed to date.

Career services

YouthNet Career Development Centre (YCDC)

Training and development programmes and workshops that prepare young people for the world of work, from employability skills to career guidance.

Skilling

YouthNet Skill Development Centre (YSDC)

Structured, sector-specific skilling — including the Skills to Succeed (S2S) programme covering retail management, employability and work skills.

Training

Beauty & Hair Training Programme

Vocational training that has helped incubate women entrepreneurs in the beauty sector, delivered in partnership with Godrej Good & Green's Salon-I initiative.

Livelihood

Banana Fibre Programme

A livelihood initiative turning banana plant waste into fibre-based products, creating income opportunities in farming communities.

Livelihood

Van Dhan Vikas Yojana

Supporting forest-based livelihoods and value addition for tribal communities under the national Van Dhan scheme.

Incubation

Made in Nagaland Livelihood Business Incubator (LBI)

Incubation support for local makers and small producers building sustainable, branded livelihood businesses under the 'Made in Nagaland' banner.
