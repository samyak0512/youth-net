Our Work / Entrepreneurship
Entrepreneurship

Incubating homegrown businesses across Nagaland

Incubation, mentorship and market linkages for founders — with a particular focus on women entrepreneurs and first-generation business owners.

Building Nagaland's startup ecosystem

YouthNet's entrepreneurship work spans the full founder journey — from first idea to seed funding to market access — anchored by the YouthNet Incubation Centre and delivered alongside national schemes and financial institutions.

In 2026, the YouthNet Incubation Centre awarded a cumulative ₹20 lakh grant to four early-stage startups under the GENESIS programme, and YouthNet co-organised the State Entrepreneurs Conclave 2026 in Dimapur, bringing founders, mentors and policymakers together to strengthen the state's entrepreneurial ecosystem.

Key initiatives

Programmes under Entrepreneurship

Incubation

YouthNet Incubation Centre (YIC)

YouthNet's core incubation programme for early-stage founders, offering mentorship, market linkages and access to seed capital.

Women founders

YouthNet Women Entrepreneurship Fellowship (YWEF)

A fellowship supporting women building and scaling their own businesses across Nagaland.

Enterprise support

Entrepreneurship Development Centre (EDC)

Structured training and handholding support for aspiring and early-stage entrepreneurs.

Seed funding

Genesis Scheme (MeitY) & Startup India Seed Fund Scheme (SISFS)

Access to national seed-funding schemes, including a ₹20 lakh cumulative grant awarded to four startups under GENESIS in 2026.

Retail & branding

Made in Nagaland (MIN) Centre

A dedicated centre helping local producers brand, package and market their goods under the 'Made in Nagaland' identity.

Women & AI

Women Economic Empowerment (WEE) Programme & AI Training for Women Entrepreneurs

Economic empowerment programming for women entrepreneurs, including new training on applying AI tools to grow small businesses.

Finance access

RAMP Nagaland & SIDBI Micro Enterprises Promotion Programme (MEPP)

Support connecting micro-enterprises to formal finance and enterprise development resources, delivered with SIDBI.

Vision 2035

Nagaland Entrepreneurship Impact Forward (NEIF) 2035

A long-horizon initiative aligning YouthNet's entrepreneurship work with the state's 2035 development goals.
