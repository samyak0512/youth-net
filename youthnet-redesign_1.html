What we do

YouthNet designs, implements and monitors youth and community development programmes across four major areas of work.

Education

Scholarships, remedial learning and school-strengthening programmes that keep children and youth in the classroom and moving forward.

Education programmes

Employment & Livelihood

Flagship initiative · since 2011

One Northeast, one heartbeat

Northeast Leaders Connect (NELC) — earlier known as Young Leaders Connect — is a landmark initiative by YouthNet that provides a common platform for young leaders and achievers from the Northeast, launched in 2011 in Kohima.

Every year, change-makers from business, politics, academia, sports, music and culture gather to plan where they'd like to see the Northeast in the years ahead — and to strategise on interventions at both the grassroots and policy level.
