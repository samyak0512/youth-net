Recruiting partners

Through the YouthNet Job Centre (YJC), we work with employers in Nagaland, other states, and overseas — in hospitality, retail, BPO, administration and skilled trades — closely coordinating with each business to understand their hiring needs before referring trained, job-ready candidates.
