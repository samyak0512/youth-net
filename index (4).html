Our story

Twenty years, in five chapters

From a single youth desk in Dimapur to a region-wide movement — the milestones that shaped how YouthNet works today.

See the full timeline →
2006

YouthNet is founded

Started in Nagaland with a single mandate: connect young people to education, skills and opportunity, one district at a time.

2011

Northeast Leaders Connect is launched

YouthNet convenes the first Young Leaders Connect in Kohima, bringing changemakers from across the Northeast to one table.

2015

Reach expands to all 17 districts

Programmes in Education and Livelihood extend into every district of Nagaland, backed by a growing field team.

2020

Relief work at regional scale

YouthNet mobilises emergency relief and continuity-of-livelihood support across the Northeast during the pandemic years.

2026

294,300 lives, and counting

Two decades on, YouthNet's work spans all 8 Northeast states — still grounded in the same district-level relationships it began with.

Flagship initiative · since 2011

One Northeast, one heartbeat

Northeast Leaders Connect (NELC) — earlier known as Young Leaders Connect — is a landmark initiative by YouthNet that provides a common platform for young leaders and achievers from the Northeast, launched in 2011 in Kohima.

Every year, change-makers from business, politics, academia, sports, music and culture gather to plan where they'd like to see the Northeast in the years ahead — and to strategise on interventions at both the grassroots and policy level.
